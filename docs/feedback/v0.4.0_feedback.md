# Feedback for vespa-18s-refdb, from release v0.4.0 in use

From nf-helminth-amplicon's `--reference_database` mode run against VESPA's public data (BioProject PRJNA944233: 12 human and 40 non-human primate faecal samples, MiSeq 2x301). Branch `claude/reference-database-mode-9016ac`, benchmark `tests/bench_vespa_public.py`, 2026-10-28.

Every record below was hit by real reads. Each problem made those reads stop at a shallower rank than they should, or at no rank at all.

---

## 1. Records to fix or remove

### 1a. One sequence, labels in different kingdoms

The release keeps these as one record each, and their taxonomy is blank from supergroup down (or entirely). In every case one member is a genome assembly (WGS contig) or culture sequence carrying another organism's 18S. That is a contaminant in the source data, not a real disagreement.

A systematic check would catch these and others not yet hit by reads. Flag every record whose members, or whose ≥99%-identical neighbours, disagree at supergroup or domain, and prioritise members from WGS or transcriptome assemblies (four-letter-prefix accessions such as `QJVE01…` or `GAGR01…`). Where one member is a directly sequenced, named isolate, its label should win over an assembly contig.

Pipeline side, for information: nf-helminth-amplicon now leaves records with no taxonomy out of the LCA rather than letting them veto it. Records with a domain only still collapse the LCA, which is correct while the label is unknown.

### 1b. Species field holds the host, not the parasite

| record | member | problem | action |
|---|---|---|---|
| `V4_a048af0a7d80` | `JN619291.1.1692_U` | genus *Leidyana* (written `Leidyana2` in the member label), species *Trachyphloeus angustisetulus*: the weevil host, not the gregarine | species should be *Leidyana* sp. |

This is a PR2 pattern worth scanning for: a parasite genus paired with a host species name. Gregarines, microsporidia and other symbionts sequenced from a named host are where it turns up.

## 2. Taxa to add

These are well-supported sequences in VESPA's samples that could be named only above genus, because the release has no record close enough. `candidate_local_sequences.fasta` beside this file holds 86 representative consensus sequences: each found in ≥2 samples with ≥200 read pairs, and 85–99% to its nearest record. Each is the first 250 and last 200 bp of the insert, joined by ten Ns. That makes them evidence of what's missing and probes to find matching full-length GenBank sequences, not full-length references themselves.

| nearest records | pairs in VESPA samples | samples | identity to nearest | candidates | suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Entamoeba hartmanni*, *E. coli* | ~230,000 | 39 | 0.93–0.99 | 29 | *Entamoeba* is under-represented: human *E. coli* and *E. hartmanni* variants, and the primate species (*E. chattoni*, *E. polecki* subtypes, *E. nuttalli*, *E. dispar*) |
| *Blastocystis* sp., *B. hominis* | ~114,000 | 45 | 0.88–0.99 | 26 | a curated subtype set (ST1–ST17 and the primate subtypes); most current records are "*Blastocystis* sp." with no subtype, so even exact matches can't name one |
| *Iodamoeba*, *Endolimax*, *Enteromonas* | ~4,400 | 3–11 | 0.72–0.99 | 2 | human gut commensals that VESPA names; its names likely came from an NCBI BLAST fallback, so GenBank should hold closer sequences |
| gregarines (*Leidyana*, *Gregarina*, *Monocystis*) | ~75,000 | 4–34 | mostly ~0.5 | 18 | probably parasites of dietary insects (NHP). Low priority. Many hits here are also a known pipeline limitation (only one read window aligns), which is being fixed separately |
| *Colpodella* / Colpodellaceae | ~10,000 | 3–14 | 0.91–1.00 | 6 | some reach 1.000 but stop above genus, because identical records of different genera exist; resolve those conflicts |
| *Howardula* (nematode) | ~2,600 | 4 | 0.96 | 2 | insect-parasitic nematode, from diet; low priority |

From the earlier design note, still open: *Teladorsagia*, *Cooperia* and *Ancylostoma*.

**Not a gap after all:** *Onchocerca* is in the release, as `V4_be8871edf2ab`, merging *O. volvulus* (`ADBW01003330`, from a WGS assembly) and *O. cervicalis* (`DQ094174`), which are identical over V4. VESPA reports *Onchocerca* in three human samples. The only filarioid reads we see (Human04) match *Dirofilaria immitis* `V4_2a33a9a8add1` exactly over 450 bp. Either VESPA's call is a near-neighbour assignment, or the *O. volvulus* member is a partial or poor sequence (it is a 1,234 bp WGS fragment). Worth checking whether that member covers the whole V4 insert.

## 3. Smaller notes

- **Resolution tables:** the release has `resolution_illumina_150x150.tsv` only. VESPA-style 2x300 runs (windows 250 and 200) would benefit from a `resolution_illumina_300x300.tsv`, since the pipeline reports each call's resolution from the table matching the read length.
- **Illumina windows:** `manifest.json`'s `illumina_windows` [132, 128] is right for 2x150. The pipeline takes other windows as parameters, so nothing is needed unless 2x300 becomes a supported default.
- **Correction to an earlier remark:** v0.4.0's `taxonomy.tsv` does **not** have a row whose domain is the literal `domain`. That was the header line in a shell count. The nine records with no taxonomy at all are real, and listed in §1a.
