# vespa18s v0.7.0: *Blastocystis* subtypes and a curation pass

97,696 records (17 added, 29 removed). 8,983 are helminth records. There are no new genomes or GenBank fetches: this release is about naming and correctness.

## What's new

- ***Blastocystis* is named to subtype.** 581 of the 653 *Blastocystis* records are now `Blastocystis_STn`, so reports can say ST1, ST2, ST3 and so on, instead of "*Blastocystis* sp." or "*B. hominis*".
  - All 45 accepted subtypes (ST1–ST17, ST21, ST23–ST49) are present.
  - Every subtyped record keeps its subtype with nanopore and with 2x150 and 2x300 Illumina.
  - Subtypes are assigned by edit distance to published full-length references (`curation/blastocystis_subtypes.tsv`, with a citation for each). The rule is: within 8% of a subtype's references, and at least 10 edits nearer than any other subtype.
  - ST42–ST44 and ST49, which come from the disputed 2% criterion (Gentekaki et al. 2026), are named and flagged.
  - "*B. hominis*" no longer appears.
- **Contaminated and mislabelled records were removed or corrected.** A new check finds records 99% or more identical over V4 whose labels disagree at class or above. Each suspect was confirmed by BLAST of its full source record. 31 members were dropped and 4 relabelled; every change is in `curation/overrides.tsv` with its evidence.
  - **The "*Eimeria* sp. T72184" study (OP407714–OP407729) is gone.** Its sequences are yeast, hazel/birch and mushroom 18S. Before this release, diet-derived reads could be named *Eimeria*.
  - **`V4_54f09b637b29` is now *Vigna radiata*.** The *Morchella* assembly contig behind it is legume 18S, so this reverses a v0.5.0 decision.
  - **Four records were relabelled:** *Angulamoeba* (filed as a red alga), *Uronema* the ciliate and *Lunella* the diatom (both clashed with same-named genera elsewhere), and *Closteriopsis*.
  - **Misidentified strains and assembly contaminants were dropped,** including *C. elegans* AC006763 and eight WGS contigs carrying another organism's 18S.
- **New species: *Trichuris incognita*.** Three V4 variants from the STPHxBioinformatics assembly, each nearest *T. suis*.

## Format

Unchanged, except that `members.tsv`'s `note` column can now read "relabelled: <reason>".
