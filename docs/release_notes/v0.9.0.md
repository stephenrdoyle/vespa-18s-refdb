# vespa18s v0.9.0: V4 from raw reads, and helminths from new genomes

97,784 records (46 added, 1 removed); 9,028 helminth records, 49,083 resolving to species. Of 122 livestock, horse, human, companion-animal and zoo helminths on our watch list, 89 now resolve to species (v0.8.0: 80) and 22 are missing (v0.8.0: 32).

## What's new

- **V4 recovered from raw reads.** Many 2010s helminth genome assemblies contain no rDNA, but their raw reads in ENA do. New scripts recover V4 directly from reads:
  - **PacBio HiFi** reads are scanned for V4, and a variant is kept only if it is at least 2% of a run's V4 reads (new `ENA_reads` layer).
  - **Short reads or noisy long reads** go through an iterative consensus that filters host reads. Tested on reads of a known species: it recovers the right V4 exactly, even from a seed with 8 planted errors.
  - **Two pre-release Tree of Life assemblies** form their own layer (`ToL_prerelease`).
- **New species:** *Bunostomum trigonocephalum*, *Cyathostoma americana* and *Strongylus vulgaris*. Several more are named through V4 variants of their own: *Cylicostephanus goldi* and *C. longibursatus*, *Cylicocyclus insigne*, *Coronocyclus labiatus*, *Calicophoron daubneyi* and ***Haemonchus placei***. *H. placei* carries two V4 variants in about equal amounts in two independent isolates; one is unique to it, so cattle *H. placei* reads now split between *Haemonchus* and *H. placei*.
- **Cyathostomins share one main V4.** Six species carry the same main V4, which v0.8.0 labelled *Cylicocyclus*. A read from *Cyathostomum*, *Cylicostephanus* or *Coronocyclus* would have been wrongly called *Cylicocyclus*; that record now stops at Strongylida.
- **Filarial worms checked against independent data.** *Onchocerca*, *Loa*, *Dirofilaria* and *Acanthocheilonema* differ by only 1–3 bases over V4. Each is now confirmed from new genomes or reads:
  - *Onchocerca* is confirmed from *O. volvulus* reads (Cameroon, Ghana, Mali), *O. lupi* HiFi reads and *O. ochengi*, which turns out identical to *O. volvulus*.
  - The single base separating *Onchocerca* from *Loa* is real, but falls between the 2x150 read windows. Only 2x301 or nanopore reads can tell the two apart.
  - An *O. volvulus* variant from Ghana is added, so its reads no longer tie with *D. repens*.
- ***Mansonella* corrected.** Four read sets agree that *M. perstans* and *M. ozzardi* share one main V4, so a call now stops at genus. The previous *M. ozzardi* record was a minor, divergent copy, and is kept as what it is. The *M. perstans* GenBank record, 3 bases off, is dropped.
- **Also confirmed:** *A. ceylanicum* (shares the common *Ancylostoma* V4), *Heligmosomoides* and *D. immitis*, from new genomes or reads.
- **A collapse fix.** Sequences from genomes and raw reads can no longer be outvoted by a rule meant for mislabelled database entries. It had hidden *Cyathostomum* and *Coronocyclus*.

## Still missing

22 watch-list species have no V4 anywhere public, neither assemblies nor genome reads: *Cooperia punctata* and *C. curticei*; *Oesophagostomum dentatum*, *O. radiatum*, *O. columbianum* and *O. bifurcum*; *Nematodirus filicollis* and *N. spathiger*; *Trichostrongylus vitrinus*; *Bunostomum phlebotomum*; *Strongylus edentatus*; *Anoplocephala magna*; *Strongyloides westeri*; *Ancylostoma braziliense* and *A. tubaeforme*; *Capillaria aerophila* and *C. obsignata*; and the primate parasites *Ternidens*, *Pterygodermatites*, *Molineus*, *Enterobius anthropopitheci* and *Prosthenorchis*.

## Format

Unchanged from v0.8.0. Two new source names appear in `members.tsv`: `ENA_reads` and `ToL_prerelease`.

shasum -a 256 vespa18s_$V.tar.gz
